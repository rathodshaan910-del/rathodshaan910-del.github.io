Shorts Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uploads videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channels and reads basic channel and video details (channel ID, video status) to confirm each upload went to the right channel.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data with anyone.
  • It does not access other people's accounts or data.
  • It does not use YouTube data for advertising.

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
